# Fullscreen Media Presentation

## Overview
Fullscreen mode expands media playback across the entire monitor, hiding all chrome, menus, and sidebars.

## Entering & Exiting
- Press `F` or `F11` to enter fullscreen.
- Press `Escape` or `F` to exit fullscreen and return to standard windowed layout.
- Controls automatically hide after 2 seconds of mouse inactivity during fullscreen playback.
