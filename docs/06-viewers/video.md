# Video Player

## Overview
The native Video Player delivers hardware-accelerated playback for common desktop video containers.

## Visual Interface
![Video Player Interface](../08-media/screenshots/media-video-player.png)
*Figure 06.7: Hardware-accelerated video player with scrubbing bar and audio volume slider.*

## Features
- **Playback Controls**: Play/Pause (`Space`), Seek `±5s` (`Left`/`Right`), Fullscreen (`F`).
- **Volume & Mute**: Smooth volume curve with `M` mute toggle.
- **Playback Rate**: Variable speeds from 0.25x to 2.0x.
