# Privacy & Security Settings

## Overview
Verify local-first privacy commitments.

## Guarantees
- **Zero Telemetry**: No tracking identifiers or network pings.
- **Local Analytics**: All usage history remains on your local machine.
- **Clear History**: One-click purge of recent files, search history, and path caches.
