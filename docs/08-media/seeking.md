# Seeking & Scrubbing Controls

## Overview
Flux Files provides frame-accurate scrubbing across video and audio timelines.

## Features
- **Hover Preview**: Hovering over the seek bar displays the exact timestamp.
- **Frame Step**: Press `.` (period) to step forward one frame; press `,` (comma) to step backward one frame.
