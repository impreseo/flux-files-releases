# Moving Files & Folders

## Overview
Moving transfers files or folders to a new location. Flux Files supports moving via Cut/Paste keyboard workflows or direct drag-and-drop.

## Drag & Drop Moving
- Dragging items within the **same drive volume** performs a **Move** by default.
- Dragging items across **different drive volumes** performs a **Copy** by default.
- Hold `Shift` during drag to force a Move.
- Hold `Ctrl` during drag to force a Copy.
