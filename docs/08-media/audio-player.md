# Audio Player & Waveform Visualizer

## Overview
The Audio Player renders acoustic waveforms and ID3 tag metadata for lossless and compressed audio formats.

## Visual Interface
![Audio Player Waveform](./screenshots/media-audio-player.png)
*Figure 08.3: Waveform visualizer with real-time playback position indicator.*

## Features
- **Waveform Seek**: Click anywhere on the rendered waveform to jump directly to that point in the track.
- **ID3 Metadata**: Displays Track Title, Artist, Album, Year, and embedded album cover art.
