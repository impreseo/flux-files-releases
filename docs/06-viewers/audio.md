# Audio Player

## Overview
The Audio Player renders audio waveforms, ID3 metadata tags (artist, album, track, year), and album art.

## Visual Interface
![Audio Player Waveform](../08-media/screenshots/media-audio-player.png)
*Figure 06.8: Audio Player featuring interactive waveform visualizer and playback scrubber.*

## Supported Formats
MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, AAC, M4A.

## Controls
- Click anywhere on the waveform visualizer to jump directly to that timestamp.
- Spacebar toggles playback; `Up`/`Down` adjusts volume.
