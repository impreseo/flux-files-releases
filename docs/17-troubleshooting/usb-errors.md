# USB & Removable Media Troubleshooting

## Overview
Resolving USB drive detection and ejection issues.

## Common Scenarios
### 1. "USB Drive Not Appearing"
- **Cause**: Drive has no assigned drive letter in Windows Disk Management.
- **Solution**: Assign a drive letter in Windows Disk Management or unplug and reinsert.

### 2. "Drive Cannot Be Ejected"
- **Cause**: A file on the USB drive is currently open in Flux Files or another program.
- **Solution**: Close all tabs pointing to the USB drive and click **Eject** again.
